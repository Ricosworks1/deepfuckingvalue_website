Commandment x says spread joy and humour above all. Here are the tools. Everything happens on your device — nothing is uploaded, nothing is saved.

Nothing leaves your browser. When you pick a picture it is read directly into the page and drawn onto the canvas. There is no upload, no server, and no storage — this page cannot make a network request at all.

Video works the same way. The clip is recorded from the canvas in your browser with MediaRecorder — a built-in browser feature — and saved straight to your device. MP4 where your browser supports it, WebM otherwise.

Every meme you download is also kept in your meme history — stored in this browser only, never uploaded.

Made something good? Post it and tag us. The best ones get shared.

The DFV cat is our mark — use it for DFV memes freely. Please don't use it to impersonate the project, and never attach it to a claim link.
