The raffle

Ten thousand tickets at 100 USDC. One hundred winners drawn by Chainlink — a Rolex and ninety-nine cash prizes of 4,000 USDC. When the last ticket sells, the draw happens on its own. Nobody can stop it, delay it, or choose who wins.

Not open yet. The contract has been built and rehearsed in full on Ethereum mainnet, but it has not been audited, and it will not take a single real ticket until it has been. When it opens, this page becomes the place to enter.

In the meantime you can read exactly what the rehearsal did — a complete round with real money, every transaction public.

It draws itself

The moment the last ticket sells, or six months after the first one, the draw triggers. Anyone can trigger it, and a keeper does it automatically — so it does not depend on us being awake, or willing.

Nobody picks the winners

Chainlink VRF produces the randomness with a proof that is checked on-chain before the contract can use it. We deliberately do not use block data, which a miner could re-roll.

There is no admin

No owner, no pause, no upgrade, no withdrawal function. The house address is fixed at deployment and no code path sends USDC anywhere else. Nothing can be changed afterwards — including by us.

You can always take your prize

Prizes are paid out in batches automatically, but if that ever stops, a winner can pull their own prize directly from the contract at any time. It does not expire.

Where the money goes

40.8% of everything collected is paid back out as prizes — the watch plus the ninety-nine cash prizes. The rest is the house's. That share is fixed in the contract and cannot be changed.

This holds whether the round fills or not. A round that sells nine thousand tickets pays the same 40.8%, scaled down, so nobody gains from a round failing to fill. Below 500 tickets the round is cancelled instead and buyers take back 80% of what they paid.

Be clear-eyed about this. A raffle is not an investment. Most tickets win nothing, and the expected return on a ticket is less than what you paid for it. Only buy what you are happy to lose.

It has already been run, with real money

Before opening, we ran a complete round on Ethereum mainnet at one fifty-thousandth of the size — real USDC, real Chainlink randomness, our own money. Two thousand tickets, one hundred winners, every prize delivered, nothing left in the contract.
