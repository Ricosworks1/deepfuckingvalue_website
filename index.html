What is DFV

A meme with receipts.

DFV takes its name from the most famous act of conviction in retail investing history — holding when every incentive said sell. It is a meme coin, and it is not shy about that. What it refuses to be is the other kind of meme coin: the one with a hidden team wallet, an unannounced unlock, and a founder quietly exiting into his own community.

So the tokenomics were built the other way round. There is no team allocation. There is no private round waiting to dump. There is no mint function, so the supply that exists is the supply that will always exist. 84.51% of every DFV token was placed into a public Uniswap pool at launch, where anyone can see it and anyone can trade against it.

The 15% that isn't liquid belongs to the Blind Believers — the people who backed this before there was anything to back. It is locked in a vesting contract for five years, with nothing at all released in year one. Governance runs on-chain through a DAO with a timelock, so no decision takes effect the moment it passes.

Vesting

How to claim.

Claiming is only for the 30 Blind Believer addresses in the vesting contract. If your wallet isn't one of them, there is nothing to claim here — and anyone telling you otherwise is trying to rob you.

Step 1

Check before you connect

The vesting contract has a public read function, getClaimableAmount. You can call it on Etherscan with your address and see exactly what's available — no wallet connection, no signature, no risk.

Step 2

Wait for the cliff

Nothing unlocks in the first year. Zero percent is released at the start, and after the one-year cliff your allocation begins releasing continuously — second by second — across five years.

Step 3

Call claim

When you have a balance available, claim() sends it to your wallet. Tokens are never pushed to you automatically; they wait in the contract until you ask for them.

Good to know

Someone else can pay the gas

claimFor(address) lets anyone trigger a claim on a beneficiary's behalf. The tokens always go to the beneficiary — never to the caller — so there is no way to redirect them.

There is no claim deadline and no rush. Vesting releases on its own schedule whether you claim daily or once in five years. Any "claim now or lose it" message is a scam — urgency is the oldest trick there is.
